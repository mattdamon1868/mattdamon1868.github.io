As a Field Engineer II at Honeywell Process Solutions in San Bruno, I served as the principal technical expert on HVAC systems, with a focus on the integration and maintenance of commercial Fire Alarm, Security, and Access Control systems.

I led troubleshooting and resolution of startup issues, using advanced diagnostic skills to ensure optimal equipment performance and reliability, and developed and programmed building management controls using Honeywell software to improve operational efficiency across deployments.

I also led instructional sessions for subcontractors on electrical and mechanical system installations and mentored junior field service representatives in technical skills, customer service, and troubleshooting techniques.
