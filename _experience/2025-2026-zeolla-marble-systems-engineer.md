As a Systems Engineer at Zeolla Marble, I deployed and configured imaging software that reduced design iteration time by 40%, saving roughly two hours per marble slab and streamlining the shop's AutoCAD-based fabrication workflow.

I designed the network infrastructure and camera integration pipeline for real-time capture and processing, and used Onshape to design parts fabricated from carbon steel for the project.

I collaborated with a cross-functional team, including a networking and communications specialist and a fabrication engineer, to transition the shop from a legacy manual workflow to an automated system design.
