As of yesterday, July 27th, 2026, after several years of research and over a year and a half of editing and review, my paper has been officially published in the IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology journal.

A great deal of hard work went into this project, including numerous hours of research, design and testing as well as multiple iterations of the article. I was fortunate to work with several colleagues from my research lab at San Diego State University (DSIM) who contributed significantly to making this publication possible. My co-author Patrick McNamee was instrumental in developing the theory, we spent many days staring at a whiteboard collaborating and reviewing the math concepts. I couldn't have done it without his constant support. Qixu Wang assisted with testing and editing, providing valuable insights. Zahra Nili Ahmadabadi, PhD, my mentor and co-author, offered ongoing feedback and encouraged my growth as a researcher. And thanks to Nicholas Calkins for his assistance with testing and designing the mobile robot used in our experiments.

The dedication behind this work cannot be fully conveyed in words, but I would like to thank everyone involved. I hope our lab continues to produce impactful and novel research going forward.
