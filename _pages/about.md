I'm a robotics engineer with a background in mechatronics and mechanical engineering spanning controls, autonomous systems, and computer vision, most recently a Graduate Research Assistant at the [DSIM Lab](https://dsimlab.com/) at San Diego State University. My work centers on adaptive control approaches for autonomous mobile robots, in particular extremum seeking control (ESC) for source-seeking behavior in nonholonomic vehicles.

Before SDSU, I worked as a Field Engineer II at Honeywell Process Solutions, and I've also designed robotics and computer vision curriculum as a freelance course designer for [The Construct](https://www.theconstruct.ai/).
