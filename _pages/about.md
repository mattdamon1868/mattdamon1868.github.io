I'm a robotics engineer with a background in mechatronics and mechanical engineering spanning controls, autonomous systems, and computer vision. I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the [DSIM Lab](https://dsimlab.com/) at San Diego State University. My work centers on adaptive control approaches for autonomous mobile robots, in particular extremum seeking control (ESC) for autonomous systems, AMR, AUV, ASV. My professional interests in the robotics industry encompass autonomous systems, motion control, and robotics systems engineering.

Before graduate school, I worked as a Field Engineer II at [Honeywell](https://www.honeywell.com/us/en) where I worked on building automation control. Currently I am working for [Clear Blue Sea](https://www.clearbluesea.org/) as a Autonomy Engineer. They are a 501c organization on a mission to "clean up waterways and prevent further pollution reaching the ocean." Their main ASV titled FRED or *Floating Robot for Eliminating Debris* has a conveyor mechanism to patrol the busy water ways collecting debris and waste. As an Autonmy engineer I am working on designing the autonomy stack for our other robot titled "micro-FRED". 

Outside of my career, I am a fourth-generation San Franciscan and have lived all over California, from sunny San Diego to the foggy Mendocino coast. I enjoy trail running and going to the gym as a daily ritual, and I also like golf and basketball. When I’m not being active, I work on side projects or try new restaurants since I love exploring different cuisines. Right now, I live in North Los Angeles and I’m looking for opportunities throughout California. If you’re an engineer or a fellow robotics researcher, I’d be happy to connect and collaborate.
