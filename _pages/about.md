I'm a robotics engineer with a background in mechatronics and mechanical engineering spanning controls, autonomous systems, and computer vision. I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the [DSIM Lab](https://dsimlab.com/) at San Diego State University. My work centers on adaptive control approaches for autonomous mobile robots, in particular extremum seeking control (ESC) for autonomous systems, AMR, AUV, ASV.

Before SDSU, I worked as a Field Engineer II at Honeywell Process Solutions, and I've also designed robotics and computer vision curriculum as a freelance course designer for [The Construct](https://www.theconstruct.ai/). My professional interests in the robotics industry encompass autonomous systems, motion control, and robotics systems engineering.

A brief about myself: I am a fourth generation San Franciscan and have lived throughout California, from sunny San Diego to the foggy Mendocino coastline. Being a lifelong Californian, I am happy to provide recommendations for interesting locations across the state. I integrate running and workouts into my daily routine, and I also enjoy golf and basketball. Currently, I am based in the North Los Angeles and I am exploring opportunities throughout California. If you are an engineer or fellow robotics researcher, I would be pleased to connect and collaborate.
