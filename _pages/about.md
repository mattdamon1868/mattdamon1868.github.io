I'm a robotics engineer with a background spanning controls, autonomous systems, and computer vision, most recently a Graduate Research Assistant at the [DSIM Lab](https://www.sdsu.edu) at San Diego State University. My work centers on adaptive control approaches for autonomous mobile robots, in particular extremum seeking control (ESC) for source-seeking behavior in nonholonomic vehicles.

Before SDSU, I worked as a Field Engineer II at Honeywell Process Solutions, and I've also designed robotics and computer vision curriculum as a freelance course designer for [The Construct](https://www.theconstruct.ai/).

Note: for projects and work experience throughout this site, everything is ordered from present to past.
