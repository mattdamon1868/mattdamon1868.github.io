As an undergraduate senior capstone project, a team of four of us built a robot designed to autonomously navigate a client's yard, identify pet waste, and collect and dispose of it. With limited prior experience in computer vision and autonomous navigation, the project pushed us to rapidly acquire new technical skills.

My focus was the waste pickup mechanism and the computer vision detection pipeline. We engineered a 3D-printed "spike" puck that punctures waste and deposits it into an onboard container. The vision component used a convolutional neural network trained on roughly 10,000 images of pet waste, reaching about 89% classification accuracy.

**Skills:** Teamwork, CAD, coding, Python, deep learning, computer vision, fabrication, electronics, debugging
