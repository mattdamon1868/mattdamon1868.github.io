Teaching Assistant for a course on the design and implementation of control systems for robotics, covering forward and inverse kinematics, dynamics, sensor integration, and actuator control. Supported students through hands-on labs and projects using MATLAB and ROS to model and simulate robotic behaviors, and collaborated with the instructor on course materials and exercises.
